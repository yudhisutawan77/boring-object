A one-line review of the most boring everyday object: the rubber band.
It held your whole life together for years, then dried out and snapped —
and was replaced by a younger one doing the exact same nothing.

Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-xvader-the-most-boring-object-reviewed-in-one-line-bd3117c3)
bounty **"The Most Boring Object, Reviewed in One Line"**.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` writes the review (one line, 153 characters) to `$ENTRY_OUT`,
`$ENTRY_OUT_DIR/entry.txt`, or `/output/entry.txt` by default.
